# Sound Effect Assets

Place the following MP3 files here:

- `card_play.mp3` — Card play sound
- `card_capture.mp3` — Card capture/match sound
- `sweep.mp3` — Sweep (싹쓸이) sound
- `go.mp3` — Go decision sound
- `stop.mp3` — Stop decision sound
- `win.mp3` — Victory sound
- `lose.mp3` — Defeat sound
- `tap.mp3` — Button tap sound

Recommended: Free sound effects from freesound.org or mixkit.co
